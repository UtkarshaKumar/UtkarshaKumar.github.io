  • Audited the client's existing B2B platform with leadership before proposing scope, and designed the discovery sequence, templates, and topic calendar. The definition a 250-person delivery organisation builds against.
  • Four regional markets wanted their own fork. Costed three years of divergence, won the global product sponsor, and defined one core instead: 120+ capabilities across 11+ sites, each decision setting what a market could and could not extend.
  • Held every functional change against a customer problem before it entered the MVP. One alignment pass on later-phase requirements took 10,000+ hours off the timeline.
  • Reversed the production-defect crisis by restructuring delivery into cross-functional pods, then stayed until the fix held. Open defects fell from 380 to 38. Still takes the tickets that cannot wait, including a market-wide outage closed the same day.
  • On the 95-country Commercetools replatform, one commerce core sat behind the global brand site and 95+ regional distributor sites, with one shopping experience for distributor, preferred-member, and retail buyers. Diagnosed distributor churn as time to first customer and first earning after signup, and shipped recommendation engines and a guided wellness quiz. Average order value rose 15%. Distributor retention rose 40%.
  • Led the functional shift that made the client merchant of record, defining requirements across transaction lifecycle, compliance, and payment orchestration so the business took ownership of the transaction and its tax obligations across EMEA, APAC, and North America.
  • Selects the platform when the brief names an objective and no technology. On five pursuits in 2025 and 2026, that choice was made from documented selection criteria. On one, benchmarked the incumbent portal against a 137-requirement matrix, re-checked a costing model nobody had asked to be re-checked, and found 912 hours of backend and integration work zeroed out under one option, closing an apparent 28% gap to 3.6% before it reached the client.
  • On another pursuit, priced both routes for consolidating three legacy commerce sites against a 163-requirement set, and put both in front of the client, including the route they had not already chosen.
  • Built and measured a GenAI adoption programme for the 250-person organisation: Jira Rovo Chat and five custom agents, each measured against a baseline. Story creation ran 50 to 80% faster, bug analysis 30% faster, design work 30% faster, and quality gains were 40 to 50%. The rollup was a 19% efficiency gain.
