Deloitte Digital, Senior Product Manager
2022 to present · Bengaluru · A rebuild across 95 markets from 2022 to 2024, then platform strategy and delivery through July 2025, followed by hypercare and BA/QA leadership.
I owned discovery, delivery, and core product strategy for an 11-market B2B platform through July 2025, then led BA and QA through hypercare after the client took over product ownership.
  • Before proposing what to build, I walked the client's leaders through what the live B2B platform actually did. A wishlist of features became a statement of the problem, and the client funded the full build. SAP Commerce Cloud, 11 markets, now on its fourth release. After the client took over product ownership, I led the BA and QA function through hypercare, with two junior BAs refining requirements with product and QA teams defining the manual and automation strategy.
  • A change entered the first release only if it solved a customer problem. One review of the later requirements kept 10,000+ hours out of that release.
  • I rebuilt delivery into small cross-functional teams and stayed until the fix held. Open defects fell from 380 to 38. One market-wide outage was found and closed the same day.
  • On the 95-country rebuild, new distributors were waiting too long for a first customer and a first earning. I shipped recommendation engines and a guided wellness quiz. Average order value rose 15%. Distributor retention rose 40%.
  • I set the requirements for the client to become the legal seller on each sale. The business then kept the revenue and the tax, across Europe, Asia Pacific, and North America.
  • On five proposals in 2025 and 2026, the client named a goal and no system, and I chose the platform from a written set of criteria. On one, I compared the current portal against 137 requirements, then re-checked a cost model nobody had asked me to re-check. 912 hours of backend and integration work had been left out of one option. An apparent 28% cost gap closed to 3.6% before the client saw it.
  • On another proposal, I priced both ways to bring three old commerce sites together, against 163 requirements, and showed the client both, including the route they had not already picked.
  • I built and measured a GenAI programme for the 250-person delivery organisation: Jira Rovo Chat and five custom agents, each compared with how the work was done before. Drafting a user story got 50 to 80% faster, bug analysis 30% faster, design work 30% faster, and quality checks improved 40 to 50%. Across the organisation the gain was 19%.
